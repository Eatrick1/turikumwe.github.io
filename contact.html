Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Still curious about something? Reach out, we're always happy to talk it through.

How can I donate to Turikumwe?+

You can donate via mobile money, bank transfer, or in-kind (pads, soap-making materials, piglets). Fill in the contact form or call us directly and our team will guide you.

Can I visit a training session in person?+

Yes, we welcome visitors during office hours. Please contact us ahead of time so we can plan a session that's ready to show you around.

How do I book the children's dance troupe for an event?+

Send us your event date, location and details through the contact form and our coordinator will confirm availability and any performance fee that supports the children's program.

I need help, is your domestic violence support confidential?+

Yes. Our safe-space circles and counselling referrals are handled with care and discretion. Please call us directly, and we will guide you to the right support.

Can organisations or churches partner with Turikumwe?+

Absolutely, we work with local partners on training, outreach and funding. Reach out and let's explore what a partnership could look like.
